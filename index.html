Finance App: Privacy and Terms

This private finance application is operated by Simon Jobs for personal, non-commercial use only. It is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy

With the account holder's consent, the application reads account details, balances and transactions from the operator's own bank accounts through an open banking provider. The data is used only for the operator's personal budgeting, is stored on a privately operated server, is not sold or shared with other parties, and is kept until the operator deletes it. Consent can be withdrawn at any time.

Terms

The application is provided as is, without warranty, and only the operator may use it. It is read-only and does not initiate payments.
